    What to do next at the bench

    Start here

    The first experiment of each hypothesis's plan, cheapest first. Each one decides whether the rest of that plan is worth running. Select one to open its design here.

      The plan by hypothesis

      Rows are hypotheses, columns the question each experiment answers, read left to right. A double-bordered experiment serves several hypotheses; point at it to see where else it appears. Select any experiment to open its full design under that row.

      Which drugs could act on each hypothesis

      Research hypotheses for discussion, not treatment advice. Drug facts (approval, mechanism, black-box warnings) come from Open Targets; how sure each drug is to break the circuits is computed from the circuits it acts in.

      All drugs at once: how sure, and how ready

      Across: the share of the circuits' support that sits on steps the drug acts on, so a drug further right breaks the circuits whichever of them is right. Up: how close the drug is to clinical use; for a patient without a settled diagnosis, the top row means reported in one of the candidate diseases. Select a drug to jump to it.

      Drugs by hypothesis

      Drugs grouped by the hypothesis whose steps they act on, best-supported hypothesis first. Within a hypothesis, the drugs that break more of its steps, and more of the circuits overall, come first; how close a drug is to use is shown but does not set the order. A drug that acts in several hypotheses appears under each, with the others listed. Select a drug to open its full case under its row.

      How Mechanist reached this, step by step

      Every stage of the run, what it asked, what it produced, how the PI reviewed it, and every result it cites. Select a stage; select any result id to read the result itself.

      Every result the team produced

      Data queries, literature searches and drug lookups, as the tools returned them.

      The full record

      Everything the run did, in order and uncut: each agent's turn as logged, with the model's whole reply under it, and each tool call with its whole result. Open a stage to read it.

      Mechanist is a separate project that reads the skinpipe outputs of a study. Cell illustrations: NIH BioArt.
